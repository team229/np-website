What's usually going on

Most people who book a love reading already know something is off. What they don't have is language for it — or permission to trust what they've noticed.

This reading is designed for exactly that point. Not "will he text me", but: what is actually happening between you, what is each of you carrying into it, what have you been ignoring, and what would it look like to move forward — with or without this person.

What people bring to this reading

  • A breakup they can't stop replaying
  • A relationship that feels one-sided but they can't name why
  • Marriage pressure from family when they aren't sure themselves
  • A situationship that has been "almost something" for months
  • Two people they care about and no clear way to choose
  • The quiet feeling that they've already decided and are scared to admit it

What you'll actually get

  • A grounded read of where the situation currently stands
  • The other person's likely position — without pretending it's a certainty
  • What you've been avoiding looking at
  • Your realistic options, including the one you haven't considered
  • Something concrete to do next, whether that's a conversation or a decision
An honest note. This reading will not promise reconciliation, and it won't tell you someone's feelings are guaranteed. It also won't try to control another person's choices — that isn't how I work. What it will do is make the situation clear enough for you to act on.
