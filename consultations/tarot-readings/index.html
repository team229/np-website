What a tarot reading will and won't do

It will show you where a situation actually stands, what you've been avoiding looking at, and what your realistic options are. Most people leave knowing something they already half-knew, said out loud for the first time.

It won't give you a guaranteed outcome. It won't tell you what another person will definitely do, and it won't replace a lawyer, a doctor or a financial adviser. If a reading isn't the right tool for what you're facing, I'll say so rather than take the booking.
