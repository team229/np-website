What a tarot reading actually does

Tarot doesn't hand you a fixed future. It reads the dynamics of a situation as they stand right now — where the energy is stuck, what's being avoided, what the other people involved are likely carrying, and what your realistic options look like.

In practice, that means you leave the session knowing three things: what's actually going on, what you've been reluctant to look at, and what to do next. Most clients say the useful part wasn't a prediction — it was hearing something out loud that they already half-knew.
