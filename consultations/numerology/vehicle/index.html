A practical way to use this

Vehicle numerology is the least mystical of the readings and the most immediately useful, because it often informs a purchase you're about to make anyway. If you're choosing between three registration numbers, knowing which one suits your own numbers is genuinely useful information — and it costs far less than most people assume.

For an existing vehicle, the reading is more reflective: it describes the kind of relationship you tend to have with that vehicle — how much you use it, how it behaves, whether it feels like yours. Most clients find it recognisable rather than surprising.

What it won't do: predict accidents, guarantee safety, or replace servicing. If a number could prevent a crash, insurance companies would be out of business.
