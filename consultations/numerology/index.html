Why numerology is often the better entry point

Tarot asks you to bring a question. Numerology doesn't. It works from what you already have — your date of birth, your name, the number on your house or your vehicle — and describes the patterns that show up around those things.

That makes it a gentler place to start if you're not sure what to ask, or if the idea of a live reading feels intense. You get a written report you can read at your own pace, and come back with questions afterwards if you want to.

In practice, the most useful sections are almost always the personal year (what phase you're in right now) and the name number (how the name you use lands on other people). Those two explain a surprising amount of the "why does this keep happening" feeling.
