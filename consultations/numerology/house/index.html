Why a house number reads differently from a person's number

A person's number describes a life. A house number describes an atmosphere — the mood of the space, the kind of behaviour it encourages, and the friction it tends to produce. Two people can live at the same address and experience it differently, but the pattern is usually recognisable.

The most common thing this reading explains: why a home that looks perfect on paper never quite feels calm. Usually it's a mismatch between the number's natural rhythm and what the occupants need. A number 5 house is wonderful if you travel constantly and miserable if you want stillness.
