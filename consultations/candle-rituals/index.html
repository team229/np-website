What this is — and what it isn't

A candle ritual is a structured, repeated intention practice. Over a set number of days, a candle is dressed, lit and worked with a specific focus. The repetition is the point: it holds attention on one thing long enough for something to shift.

It is not a switch you flip to make another person act. It is not a substitute for a decision you're avoiding. And it is not something anyone can honestly guarantee.

What clients most often report is a change in their own clarity — sleeping better, feeling less stuck, finally making a call they'd been circling for months. That's the realistic outcome, and it's usually the one that matters.

Important. These are spiritual, intention-based practices. Outcomes are not guaranteed.
