When a standard reading isn't the right fit

Most situations fit one of the standard services. But some don't. A few examples of what people bring here:

  • A career decision tangled up with a family obligation
  • A relationship question where the real issue is money, or a business
  • A business partnership where two people want different things
  • Several separate decisions that all seem connected
  • A question they can't articulate yet and need help shaping

For these, we start with a conversation. If a standard service covers it, I'll point you there — it's cheaper and faster. If it genuinely needs something custom, I'll propose a format and a price.

What a custom session can include

  • Tarot reading combined with numerology analysis
  • A kundali view alongside a tarot read
  • Ritual planning for a complex situation
  • An extended session (90 minutes) for multiple connected questions
  • A written summary you can refer back to
An honest note. I won't take a booking I don't think will help. If the situation calls for a lawyer, a therapist or a financial adviser, I'll say so — and I won't dress that up as spiritual guidance.
