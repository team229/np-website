What a kundali reading actually covers

A birth chart is a map of where the planets sat at the moment you were born. Read well, it describes tendencies rather than events: how you handle pressure, where you tend to over-give, what kind of work suits you, and which areas of life require more deliberate effort.

The problem with most kundali readings isn't the astrology — it's the delivery. Clients come away with Sanskrit terms, a list of doshas, and a shopping list of gemstones. That's not useful, and it's often frightening for no good reason.

This reading is written in plain language. Every technical term gets explained. If something in your chart is genuinely difficult, you'll hear it directly — and you'll also hear what to do about it.

Two ways to work with your chart

Kundali Analysis — the full picture

Planetary placements, house analysis, strengths and challenges, and the life areas you asked about: career, relationships, finances, personal growth. Doshas are identified only where relevant and always explained.

Varshfal — the year ahead

A focused annual reading. Major themes for the coming year, career and relationship trends, the periods worth pushing in, and the periods worth protecting. Most useful booked in December or January, but it works at any point in the year.

An honest note. I don't sell gemstones and I don't recommend expensive remedies. If the chart supports a simple practice — a mantra, a donation, a small ritual — I'll say so. If it doesn't, I'll say that too. Nobody should leave a reading feeling they must spend money to be safe.
