Disclaimer

What these services are, what they aren't, and what you're agreeing to. Written plainly, because it matters.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Guidance, not guarantees

Tarot readings, numerology, Vedic astrology and intention-based rituals are offered as tools for reflection and personal insight. They are not predictions of the future, and no outcome is guaranteed. Any decision you make after a consultation is your own.

Not medical, legal or financial advice

Nothing on this website or in any consultation constitutes medical, psychological, legal or financial advice. These services are not a substitute for treatment, diagnosis, legal counsel or professional financial planning. If you are facing a medical, legal or financial issue, please consult a qualified professional.

Mental health

A reading can offer emotional clarity, but it is not therapy and does not treat any condition. If you are experiencing significant distress, please contact a licensed mental health professional or a crisis helpline in your area. In India, Tele-MANAS is available on 14416.

Rituals and consent

All ritual work is intention-based spiritual practice. Rituals involving relationships respect the autonomy and consent of everyone involved. Requests to influence another person against their will are declined.

Ritual outcomes are not guaranteed. What is offered is consistent, properly conducted practice — not a promised result.

Products

Ritual oils and crystals are sold as items for personal spiritual practice. They are not medicines and make no medical, psychological or supernatural claim.

  • Oils are for external use only. Do not ingest.
  • Patch-test on a small area before first use.
  • Keep away from children and avoid contact with eyes and broken skin.
  • If pregnant, nursing, or managing a health condition, consult your doctor before use.
  • Natural stones vary in colour, pattern and shape. No two pieces are identical.

Age

Services and products are intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Confidentiality

Everything shared in a consultation is treated as confidential. Client messages shown on Instagram are shared by those clients themselves, with permission, and personal details are removed. Sessions may not be published or redistributed without consent.

Payments, cancellations and refunds

  • Sessions are confirmed on payment.
  • Rescheduling is free with at least 4 hours' notice.
  • Sessions are non-refundable once delivered.
  • Products: opened oils and worn crystals cannot be returned for hygiene reasons. Damaged or incorrect items are replaced or refunded if reported within 48 hours with a photograph.

Third-party platforms

Bookings are coordinated over WhatsApp and payments made by UPI or bank transfer. Those platforms have their own terms, and their availability is outside our control.
