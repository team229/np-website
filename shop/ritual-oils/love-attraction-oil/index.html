Love Attraction Oil is about your side of the equation. The premise is simple: most people who struggle in relationships are not short of opportunities — they're short of the belief that they deserve a good one.

It's dressed with rose and soft floral botanicals in a light base oil. The scent is gentle and warm.

Common intentions

  • Working on self-worth after a relationship has ended
  • Opening to connection rather than chasing it
  • Softening after a period of defensiveness
  • A daily ritual of treating yourself with some care

What it isn't: a way to make a specific person want you. That isn't how this works, and I won't sell it that way.

Sizes: 10ml and 20ml.

Before you start: patch-test on your inner wrist.

  1. Anoint: a drop on each wrist, or over the heart, as part of a morning routine.
  2. Or dress a candle: a thin line down a pink or white candle, then set an intention about what you're opening to — not about a person.
  3. Keep it about you. The useful intention is "I'm willing to be seen", not "he will come back".

Store away from direct sunlight. External use only. Do not ingest.
