Protection Oil is for the after, not the before. Once a space or a situation has been cleared, protection is what holds it steady — the difference between cleaning a room and locking the door.

It's dressed with resinous, grounding botanicals in a base oil, and comes in a brass-capped dropper bottle. The scent is warm and slightly smoky — closer to incense than perfume.

Common intentions

  • Holding boundaries around a demanding workplace or family situation
  • Grounding before or after a difficult conversation
  • Feeling less affected by other people's moods
  • Closing a ritual or cleansing practice

Sizes: 10ml (roughly 2–3 months of regular use) and 20ml.

Before you start: patch-test a small amount on your inner wrist and wait an hour.

  1. Anoint: one drop on each wrist, or behind the ears, before entering a situation that tends to drain you.
  2. Or dress a candle: a thin line down the candle, away from you, then light it and hold your intention for a few minutes.
  3. Set the intention out loud. The oil is a cue, not the mechanism — naming what you're holding steady is the part that does the work.

Store away from direct sunlight. External use only. Do not ingest.
