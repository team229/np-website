How to choose between them

Most people don't need more than one to begin with. The useful question isn't "which oil is strongest" — it's "what am I actually working on right now".

  • If something feels heavy and you can't name why — start with clearing, not protection. Clearing first, then protecting, is the order that makes sense.
  • If you're rebuilding after a setback — protection is for holding your energy steady while you do that.
  • If money is the focus — Money Oil is for directing attention at opportunity, not for making money appear.
  • If you're working on self-worth and openness — Love Attraction is about your own capacity to receive, not about a specific person.

If that still doesn't settle it, message me and describe the situation. I'd rather point you to the right one than sell you three.
