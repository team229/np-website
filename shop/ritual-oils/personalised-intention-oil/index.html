What you get

Before you start: patch-test on your inner wrist and wait an hour.

  1. Read the note first. The blend is built around one intention — knowing what it is makes the practice more deliberate.
  2. Anoint or dress a candle. A drop on each wrist, or a thin line down a candle away from you.
  3. Name the intention. The oil is a cue. Naming what you're working toward is the part that does the work.

Store away from direct sunlight. External use only. Do not ingest.
