Money Oil is the most misunderstood product in the collection, so let's be precise: it does not attract money. What it does is create a deliberate moment around financial decisions — which, for most people, is exactly the missing piece.

It's dressed with warm, resinous botanicals and a touch of citrus in a golden base oil. The scent is bright and slightly sweet.

Common intentions

  • Starting a new month or a new financial cycle deliberately
  • Before a negotiation, pitch or pricing conversation
  • Business rituals around client attraction and growth
  • Shifting out of a scarcity mindset that's driving bad decisions

Sizes: 10ml and 20ml.

Before you start: patch-test on your inner wrist.

  1. Anoint: a drop on each wrist before a workday, a negotiation, or a financial decision.
  2. Or dress a candle: a thin line down the candle, then set one specific financial intention — a number, a goal, a conversation you've been avoiding.
  3. Name it concretely. "More money" does nothing. "Send the invoice I've been putting off" does something.

Store away from direct sunlight. External use only. Do not ingest.
